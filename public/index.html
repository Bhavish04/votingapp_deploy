About Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir

Sri Sri Ravishankar Vidya Mandir (SSRVM), Mangalore, is a CBSE-affiliated institution dedicated to providing world-class holistic education that nurtures the cognitive, emotional, physical, social, and spiritual growth of every student. Guided by the vision of Gurudev Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, the school combines academic excellence with strong human values, creativity, leadership, and social responsibility.

At SSRVM, learning is designed to be joyful, experiential, and stress-free. Students are encouraged to broaden their vision and deepen their roots by appreciating Indian culture and values while developing a global perspective. Through academics, sports, yoga, arts, music, co-curricular activities, and value-based education, the school empowers students to become confident, compassionate, innovative, and socially conscious citizens.

This Election Management System reflects SSRVM's commitment to nurturing leadership, responsibility, democratic values, and active student participation, helping young minds develop the confidence and integrity required to become future leaders.
